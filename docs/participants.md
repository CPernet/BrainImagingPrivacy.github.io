## Three useful questions

- **What information will the study collect about you?** Ask about scans or recordings and the other information collected alongside them.
- **Who may use that information?** Ask about the study team, other researchers and any public sharing.
- **Who can answer your questions?** Keep the study team's contact details and participant information sheet.

## How will the study protect the data?

Ask the research team to explain its safeguards in everyday language, including who can access the data and what happens if they are shared.

Useful questions include:

- Will my name and contact details be kept separately from my research data?
- Will any information be made publicly available?
- Will other researchers need permission to obtain the data?
- How long will the data be kept?
- What are the limitations of the protections used?

## Choices and study contacts

Ask what choices are available in the specific study, including what happens if you decide to stop participating or change a data-sharing choice. The team should explain any limits, particularly for information that has already been shared or used.

This website provides general information. Your study's participant information sheet and research team are the starting points for questions about your own participation.
