## Questions for policy discussions

- **What has been demonstrated?** Specify the data, methods and conditions involved.
- **Where does the evidence apply?** Identify the populations, settings and assumptions covered.
- **What are the consequences of a proposed safeguard?** Consider protection, scientific use and implementation together.

## Understand the scope

Brain imaging privacy involves acquired images and recordings, associated metadata, individual derivatives and summary results. The planned briefs will make their data scope explicit rather than treating all research outputs as interchangeable.

## Read the evidence

The website will connect concise explanations to papers, expert assessments and reproducible analyses.

- [Assessing metadata privacy in neuroimaging](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.1144): a published study of metadata accompanying OpenNeuro datasets.
- [Expert-ratings project](https://github.com/CPernet/BrainImagingPrivacy.github.io/tree/main/papers/expert-ratings): ongoing work on identification and singling-out likelihood across neuroimaging data types.

**Planned content:** short evidence briefs separating empirical findings, expert judgement and unresolved questions.

## Consider policy implications

Future briefs will examine how safeguards relate to research settings, access models and scientific needs. Recommendations will state their supporting evidence and the scope of expert agreement.

No consensus policy recommendations are presented in this initial outline.

## Keep assessments current

The site is designed to accept new evidence and corrections through community review. Changes to guidance will include a dated explanation and links to the evidence considered.

**Planned content:** an update history and a documented process for reassessing the published likelihood ratings.

## Help shape the resource

Policy contributors can suggest questions, identify unclear terminology and submit relevant evidence. Legal or regulatory sources should include their jurisdiction, date and current status.
