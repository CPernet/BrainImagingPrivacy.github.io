## Questions to start with

- **What will be shared?** Describe the images, recordings, metadata and derived results involved.
- **Who will have access?** Document the intended recipients and access arrangements.
- **What evidence informs the assessment?** Identify the relevant findings, assumptions and uncertainties.

## Identify the data involved

The research workflow can involve several distinct kinds of information. The planned guidance will address each explicitly.

| Data category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Acquired data | MRI, CT, PET, EEG and MEG |
| Associated metadata | Demographics, acquisition information and clinical measurements |
| Individual derivatives | Statistical maps, surfaces and connectivity matrices |
| Summary results | Group maps, aggregate tables and figures |

## Assess the evidence

The expert-ratings project examines re-identification and singling-out across data types. Its current analysis is available in the [paper folder](https://github.com/CPernet/BrainImagingPrivacy.github.io/tree/main/papers/expert-ratings).

For tabular metadata, the [metaprivBIDS paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.1144) and [OpenNeuro analysis notebooks](https://github.com/CPernet/OpenNeuro_MetaPrivAssessment) provide a worked example of privacy assessment.

**Planned content:** reviewed summaries by data type, assessment scenarios and the limitations of available evidence.

## Consider safeguards

The guidance will cover technical measures, access arrangements and governance together. Worked examples will describe both the intended protection and the consequences for scientific use.

**Planned content:** metadata transformations, image processing, access control, agreements and documented reassessment. No particular method is endorsed by this initial outline.

## Tools and resources

- [metaprivBIDS](https://github.com/CPernet/metaprivBIDS): software for assessing privacy in tabular metadata.
- [Open Brain Consent](https://open-brain-consent.readthedocs.io/en/stable/): consent and data-sharing resources.
- [Expert-ratings analysis](https://github.com/CPernet/BrainImagingPrivacy.github.io/tree/main/papers/expert-ratings): work in progress supporting the consensus project.
