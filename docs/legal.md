## Three questions for the assessment

- **Which data and processing operations are involved?** Describe the actual information, not only the modality name.
- **What is the relevant identification scenario?** Make the actors, available information and assumptions explicit.
- **What supports the technical claims?** Link the assessment to evidence and record its limitations.

## Understand the imaging context

The site will explain acquired images and recordings, metadata, individual derivatives and group summaries. Each explanation will describe scientific uses and the information retained by processing steps.

**Planned content:** an illustrated technical glossary and examples that researchers and legal professionals can use together.

## Distinguish the questions being asked

The consensus project addresses re-identification and singling-out likelihood. These technical assessments will be described separately from jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions.

**Planned content:** reviewed terminology covering identification, singling out, linkage and inference, with clear sources and scope.

## Examine evidence and safeguards

The [expert-ratings analysis](https://github.com/CPernet/BrainImagingPrivacy.github.io/tree/main/papers/expert-ratings) is work in progress. It will support discussion of how assessments vary by data type and scenario.

The [metadata privacy paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.1144) and its [analysis notebooks](https://github.com/CPernet/OpenNeuro_MetaPrivAssessment) provide a separate empirical resource.

**Planned content:** evidence tables documenting study conditions, relevant safeguards, scientific trade-offs and unresolved questions.

## Legal context and review

Jurisdiction-specific interpretations will be identified and reviewed by appropriate legal contributors before publication. This initial page makes no determination that a particular dataset is anonymous or that a proposed use is lawful.

For primary guidance, consult the relevant authority, including the [European Data Protection Board's health and research resources](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/specific-domains/health-and-research_en).

## Contribute a worked example

Useful examples describe the dataset, proposed use, access arrangements, technical evidence and question requiring clarification. Submit an example through GitHub without including confidential information or participant-level data.
